# FlowMetrics Product Catalog

**Company:** FlowMetrics — workflow automation & analytics for operations teams.

---

## Product 1: FlowCore
**Solves:** Manual, repetitive operational tasks (data entry, approvals, status updates).
**What it does:** No-code workflow builder that automates multi-step approval chains and task handoffs across teams.
**Best for:** Companies with >20 employees drowning in manual handoffs between departments.
**Pricing:** Starts at $299/month (up to 15 seats).

## Product 2: FlowInsights
**Solves:** Lack of visibility into where operational bottlenecks actually are.
**What it does:** Real-time dashboard that tracks task cycle times, flags stuck workflows, and predicts delays before they happen.
**Best for:** Ops managers who are "flying blind" on team throughput.
**Pricing:** Starts at $199/month (add-on to FlowCore, or standalone).

## Product 3: FlowConnect
**Solves:** Data trapped in disconnected tools (CRM, ERP, spreadsheets).
**What it does:** Pre-built integrations (Salesforce, SAP, HubSpot, Google Sheets) that sync data bidirectionally without engineering work.
**Best for:** Teams juggling 5+ software tools with no single source of truth.
**Pricing:** Starts at $149/month per integration.

## Bundle: FlowSuite (All 3 products)
**Pricing:** $549/month (up to 15 seats) — most popular for mid-market ops teams.
